            Adjustments

              Select a slot to issue or burn the liability it holds for its child, or to change the contact, unit, or note. Amounts show two decimal places, and a burn cannot take a liability below 0.00. A slot stores the child's key, so renaming a contact keeps the link, but the name the child sees is the one signed with the slot: rename the contact and save the slot again to change it. Every change is signed by the owner key and merged by the contract. Clearing a slot records that it was cleared, so an older copy cannot bring it back.

              Runs on Freenet.
